# Project: IRDAI Compliance Document Crawler

## Overview

This project aims to build an **automated document collection pipeline** for an Agentic Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system focused on **IRDAI (Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India)** regulations.

Instead of manually downloading regulatory documents, the crawler will automatically discover, download, organize, and maintain the latest IRDAI compliance documents from the official IRDAI website.

The downloaded documents will later be cleaned, chunked, embedded, and indexed in a vector database for use in an Agentic RAG system that answers insurance regulatory and compliance questions.

---

# Objectives

The crawler should:

* Crawl the official IRDAI website.
* Automatically discover pages containing regulatory documents.
* Identify downloadable PDF documents.
* Download only authentic IRDAI PDFs.
* Avoid duplicate downloads.
* Maintain metadata for every document.
* Support incremental updates when new regulations are published.

The crawler should prioritize high-value compliance documents rather than downloading every file on the website.

---

# Documents to Collect

The crawler should prioritize collecting documents such as:

* IRDAI Regulations
* Master Circulars
* Guidelines
* Circulars
* Acts
* Exposure Drafts
* Frequently Asked Questions (optional)
* Discussion Papers (optional)

The crawler should ignore unrelated content such as:

* Recruitment notices
* Tenders
* Annual reports
* Press releases
* Speeches
* News articles
* Login portals
* Consumer grievance portals
* Administrative webpages

# End Goal

The final output of this project will be a **maintained, high-quality corpus of IRDAI regulatory documents** that automatically stays up to date and serves as the document ingestion layer for an **Agentic RAG system**. The RAG system will use this corpus to answer regulatory, compliance, and insurance-related queries with reliable, up-to-date information while minimizing manual effort in dataset collection and maintenance.

---

This crawler is intentionally designed as a modular pipeline rather than a one-off script, making it easier to maintain, extend, and explain in my thesis and technical interviews.
